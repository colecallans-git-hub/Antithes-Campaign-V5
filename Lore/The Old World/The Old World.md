The previous era/state of the world before the current age.

Taking place centuries ago, much of the knowledge of this era was lost, supposedly to the greed and rage of dragons. Few historic locations remain as proof of its existence, including:
The [[Ruins of Ávolīre]]
The [[Great Library of the Old World]]
The [[Royal Keep of the Old World]]
The [[Remains of Wray Keep]]
[[Hero's Clearing]]
and the [[Hordraine Kingdom]], though this kingdom spent much of its time actively keeping out of foreign affairs, and thus lived in peace.
