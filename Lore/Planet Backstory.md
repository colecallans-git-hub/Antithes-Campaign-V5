
Back in the beginning age of the planet's life, there lived [[Primordials]] and celestial beings, placed to shape the land air and sea under their deity creator (name undecided).
Then, a huge asteroid fell from onto the land, leaving lasting effects across the planet. Shockwaves and tremors were caused around the world, causing tectonic shifts and creating the land masses as we have them now—[[Antithes]]
The interesting thing about this incident though, is that early stages of lower life forms were introduced on this asteroid, blooming the new era of life known as the first age.
#### First Age
This first age is practically unheard of, and completely unknown to those in the current third age. Those in the third age do not even know that there was a first age, save for the fact that the entirety of society knows that it's the third age.
#### Second Age
The second age, also known as [[The Old World]], is distant to those in the third age, but they have remaining proof of such a time.
#### Third Age
The third age is actually less sophisticated than the second, and not quite to the same point of advancement as them either. This is not to say that they do not have complex societies. Political unrest is at a high, but nationalism and civic pride are also high. Many neighboring cities and kingdoms do not get along.

In each instance of a new age, all world knowledge and material were lost. The only exception were a very few select pockets around the world coming into the third age. The Primordials of the realms have been in a slumber since the end of the first age and the beginning second age. It is unknown to any of those living in the third age of Antithes, but primordial combat caused the basic destruction and regrowth of the world as a whole during the first age, going back into a slumber
