### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
A hag/oracle that has been rarely seen to live in the [[Oracle's Grotto]], located in [[The Bending Wood]].
She's a very old looking lady who keeps her eyes closed when talking. This is because she's actually blind, and has no eyes, speaking as though she sees more than any mortal. She let's no one know her true nature, but she is actually one of the sisters of fate, spinning the webs and able to read the strings of the past, present and future... For a price.
She offers those who find her place temporary sanctuary, and also offers readings in different fashions, along with means of healing, minor boons, or perhaps certain special people are overcome with visions.
