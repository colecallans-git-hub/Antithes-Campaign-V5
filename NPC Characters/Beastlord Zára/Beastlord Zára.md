### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
A human barbarian female who was cast out near the [[The Barren Edge]], left to fend for herself.
She eventually found her way to the [[Isle of Beasts]], becoming the ruler over the creatures there through a show a dominance. Having discovered and partially created the [[Throne of Beasts]], she is savage and honorable. She will test strength over trust any day.

## Hooks/Ties
Challenges PCs in the <u>Throne of Beasts</u> fighting pit. If allies are victorious (or pass her tests), she allies against primordials, viewing them as the "ultimate beasts". Suspects Veiled Assembly of selectively breeding beasts and manipulating evolutions. The rumors of the two chronovane eggs excites her as "twin beasts". If anything happens in the area, she migrates beasts to the Dragon's Tail.
