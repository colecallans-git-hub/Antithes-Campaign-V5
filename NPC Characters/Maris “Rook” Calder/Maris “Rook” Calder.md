#Character 
## Origin/Overview:
Middle-aged owner and primary barkeep of **The Dirty Gull** in [[Seastone Hall]]. Weathered, sharp-eyed, and quietly competent. She has run the tavern for years and has seen every kind of sailor, merchant, and adventurer pass through her doors.

## Hooks/Ties:
- Main face of the party’s regular tavern in [[Seastone Hall]]
- Knows a great deal of dockside gossip and can point people toward reliable information (or warn them when something feels off)
- Soft spot for those who treat her staff and regulars well
- Maintains a professional but watchful relationship with dock authorities and captains

## Sheet:
- Race: Sea Elf
- Role: Owner / Primary Barkeep
- Personality: Dry sense of humor, plain-spoken, unflappable, observant
- Appearance: Middle-aged, strong build, dark hair usually tied back, often wiping a glass or leaning on the bar
- Notable: Excellent memory for faces and drink orders; keeps the tavern running smoothly even on the busiest nights

## Attitude Toward the Party:
- Starting: Neutral / Professional
- Notes: Warms to regulars who pay their tab, cause minimal trouble, and treat the staff decently. Cools quickly toward anyone who starts fights or tries to skip payment.
