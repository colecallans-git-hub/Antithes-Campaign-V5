### #Character 

## Origin/Overview:
A young dockhand and junior crew member struggling to get by in [[Seastone Hall]]. Hardworking but often in over his head, he is currently employed on Captain Xund Wavebreaker’s ship.
## Hooks/Ties:
- Works under [[Captain Xund Wavebreaker]].
- Owes a growing debt to [[Mirene Thalor]] that he cannot pay on his own.
- Offers to help the party secure passage with Xund if they help him with the debt.
- Can become a recurring friendly contact and crew member on future voyages.
## Sheet:
- Race: Half-elf
- Role: Junior sailor / dockhand
- Personality: Earnest, anxious, loyal once trust is earned
- Notable: Knows the [[Seastone Hall]] docks well; basic sailing skills
