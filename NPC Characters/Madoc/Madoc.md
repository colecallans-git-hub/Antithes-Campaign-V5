## Origin/Overview:
The steady, practical innkeeper of the **Drowsy Captain Inn** in Seastone Hall. Broad-shouldered and quietly imposing, Madoc has spent most of his life around docks and travelers. He runs a clean, reliable house and prefers guests who pay on time and keep the noise down after dark.

## Hooks/Ties:
- Primary face of the party’s regular lodgings in Seastone Hall
- Knows many of the captains and regular travelers who pass through the city
- Can quietly recommend (or warn against) certain ships, crews, or parts of town
- Maintains a professional relationship with dock authorities and other innkeepers

## Sheet:
- Race: Dragonborn (brass or bronze ancestry preferred)
- Role: Innkeeper of the [[Drowsy Captain Inn]]
- Personality: Calm, practical, slightly gruff but fair. Not overly talkative, but reliable.
- Appearance: Middle-aged, solid build, scaled, often in a plain vest or apron; carries himself with quiet strength
- Notable: Remembers returning guests and their usual room preferences; keeps a quiet mental log of who comes and goes

## Attitude Toward the Party:
- Starting: Neutral / Professional
- Notes: Warms to guests who pay cleanly, cause little trouble, and treat the staff well. Becomes cooler toward those who bring fights or leave without settling their bill.
