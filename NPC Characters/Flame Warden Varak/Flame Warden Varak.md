### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
A fire-scarred Orc, he was a defector from the [[Citadel of the Horde]] turned enforcer for [[The Order of the Ashen Sky]].
He is seen leading raids in the [[Dagger Greenwood]] for fuel, and is stationed as main opposition against [[The Gloam Serpents]].
