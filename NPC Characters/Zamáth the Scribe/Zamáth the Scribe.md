### #Character
## Origin/Overview:
A Githzerai scholar that resides in the [[Ruins of Ávolīre]]. Working inside of [[The Veiled Assembly]], he acts as the eyes for what is generally known as the most vast collection of knowledge in the [[Great Library of the Old World]]. At times, *The Veiled Assembly* has requested that he alter historical texts with his superior expertise in order to "balance" narratives. He is very careful and methodical, seeing things as "pieces" - which makes him good at his role in the Assembly.
## Hooks/Ties
He is the guard of the Library's secrets.
