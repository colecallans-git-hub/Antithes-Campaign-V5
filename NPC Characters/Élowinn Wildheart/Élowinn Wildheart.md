### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
A female Wood Elf bonded to the spirits of the [[Faron Wilds]]. Fierce and intuitive, she speaks with animals as equals and defends the sacred land. Views change as natural but guards the balance of the wildlands.
## Hooks/Ties
Guards the [[Verdant Archive]], testing PCs with trials of the wild. Senses unnatural balances in the forests. Deeply connected, she is able feel the [[Sacred Tree Bodhisattva]] in a limited manner, which is already plenty more than anyone else. If befriended, helps in either fight against the Veiled Assembly, or in the clash between primordials.
