### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
A small librarian that is bound to the [[Wandering Scriptorium]], its knowledge rivaling that of the <u>Great Library</u>. He travels with the scriptorium to collect stories. He is a very curious hoarder, the appearance of his book and tome collection seemingly disheveled, though he knows that whereabouts of each and every piece of knowledge he stores.
## Hooks/Ties
Tower may appear near PCs at a given location.
He may offer lore on [[The Old World]] for a story trade, or something similar.
<u>The Veiled Assembly</u> has hunted him and the scriptorium for ages, unable to pinpoint it.
