### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
A dwarf that has given up many of his possessions, as well as his speech. Eyes sunken in, he is a part of [[The Hollow Tithe]], passive and resigned. Regretful of his past, he has chosen what he thought to be a path of less pain (though he has some regrets about this as well). He communicates in gestures, and seeks understanding/shared emptiness/experiences.
