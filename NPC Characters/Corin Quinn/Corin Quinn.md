## Origin/Overview:
The energetic, tomboyish clerk and day-to-day operator of **Home to Harbor**, the general goods and thrift/pawn store in [[Seastone Hall]]. Corin enjoys talking with the people who come through her doors and has a slightly scattered, restless energy. She is not particularly skilled with exact numbers and often simply decides on a price that “feels right,” though she still cares deeply about the condition of anything she buys or sells.

## Hooks/Ties:
- Main contact for the party when buying general supplies or selling unwanted gear
- Willing to purchase items from adventurers if the condition is acceptable
- Keeps informal mental notes on unusual goods and interesting customers
- Maintains casual working relationships with other merchants and the occasional captain

## Sheet:
- Race: Tiefling
- Role: Clerk / buyer at Home to Harbor
- Personality: Practical, observant, polite but not overly warm. Talkative and easily distracted in a friendly way. Slightly impulsive with pricing.
- Appearance: Young adult, tomboyish presentation, small horns, neat but lived-in work clothes; often moving while she talks
- Notable: Strong eye for condition and quality; weaker with precise accounting; will frequently say “screw it” and settle on a price that feels fair in the moment

## Attitude Toward the Party:
- Starting: Neutral / Open
- Notes: Quickly becomes friendlier toward regulars who chat and deal fairly. Remains cautious about damaged or suspicious-looking goods, even if her pricing is loose.
