### #Character 

## Origin/Overview:
A slightly tall, slender High Elf (or Sea Elf) woman with bronze skin and blonde hair. She carries herself with quiet authority and has risen to a senior position within [[The Aēlthôra]], the dock and harbor guild of [[Seastone Hall]]. Practical, composed, and exacting about debts and agreements, she serves as both a local power broker and a recurring quest-giver.
## Hooks/Ties:
- Senior figure in [[The Aēlthôra]].
- Primary quest-giver for [[Main Quest 1—The Falling Shadow]].
- Holds a **25 GP** debt from [[Fin the Dockhand]].
- Can direct the party toward Fin and, by extension, [[Captain Xund Wavebreaker]].
- Maintains working relationships with captains, merchants, and other guilds in [[Seastone Hall]] (including contacts in the Blue Lantern Company and the Iron Ledger).
- Likely to remain a regular source of local work whenever the party returns to [[Seastone Hall]].
## Sheet:
- Race: High Elf (or Sea Elf)
- Appearance: Slightly tall and slender, bronze skin, blonde hair. Late 30s to early 40s in appearance.
- Role: Senior Overseer / influential member of [[The Aēlthôra]]
- Personality: Practical, composed, fair but firm. Expects people to keep their word.
- Notable: Extensive knowledge of dock operations, debts, and the people who move through [[Seastone Hall]].
## Attitude Toward the Party:
- Starting: Neutral / Professional
- Notes: Improves if the party handles Fin’s debt cleanly and completes work reliably. Cools if they cause disruption on the docks or treat agreements carelessly.
