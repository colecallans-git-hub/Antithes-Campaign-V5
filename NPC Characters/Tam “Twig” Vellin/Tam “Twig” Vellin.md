#Character 
## Origin/Overview:
Young adult bartender and kitchen help at **The Dirty Gull** in [[Seastone Hall]]. Lean, energetic, and well-liked by the regulars. Taller than your average halfling, he handles a lot of the day-to-day serving and simple food preparation under [[Maris “Rook” Calder]]’s direction.

## Hooks/Ties:
- Secondary, friendly face at the party’s regular tavern
- Often the first person customers interact with when the bar is busy
- Good source of lighter gossip and local color
- Loyal to Maris and protective of the tavern’s reputation

## Sheet:
- Race: Halfling
- Role: Secondary Barkeep / Kitchen Help
- Personality: Friendly, talkative, eager to please, quick with a joke
- Appearance: Young, lean, constantly moving between the bar and the kitchen
- Notable: Remembers regulars’ usual orders; good at keeping the mood light

## Attitude Toward the Party:
- Starting: Friendly / Open
- Notes: Quick to warm up to polite or interesting customers. Looks to Maris for cues if trouble starts.
