### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
The Collector is the head of [[The Iron Reliquary]], said to be an ageless merchant of curiosities that has walked every land and died many times, rarely ever seen in person. He is enigmatic and an obsessive hoarder, though this doesn't discredit his intelligence.
He only talks through through his messengers, who tend to be one of [[The Reclaimers]].
