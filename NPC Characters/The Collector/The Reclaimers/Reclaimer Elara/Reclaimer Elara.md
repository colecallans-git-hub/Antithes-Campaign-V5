### #Character 
## Overview
A female half-elf merchant and scout working as one of [[The Reclaimers]] for [[The Collector]] inside [[The Iron Reliquary]]. Ambitious yet code-bound, she seeks to work her way up the ranks of her peer reclaimers. Thought not yet knowledgeable in all areas, she has very niche history information - an expertise in her specific field
Can be found in the territories in and around [[Ringris Docks]] and [[Shipwreck Strait]].
## Hooks/Ties
May approach PCs in Port Ringris for trade, reeled in by the scale. If refused, she works to steal items off of PCs (either herself or through hires). Scouts the cliff faces of Shipwreck Strait.
