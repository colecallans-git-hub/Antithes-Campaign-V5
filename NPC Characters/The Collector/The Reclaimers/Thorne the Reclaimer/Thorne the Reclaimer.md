### #Character 
## Overview
A male dwarf merchant originating from the [[Adamantine Highlands]] - he is greedy, but bound by the code set upon [[The Reclaimers]] that work for [[The Iron Reliquary]]
A more well-known and actionable reclaimer, he is seen heavily in Northern and northwestern [[Ūdeth]]
## Hooks/Ties
Meets PCs in an Ūdeth city, offering to appraise things for them for free but reports directly to [[The Collector]]. Suspects Veiled Assembly of "relic hoarding" in the Great Library. Second egg rumor creates a proposition for a potential joint hunt in the Ruins of Ávolīre.
