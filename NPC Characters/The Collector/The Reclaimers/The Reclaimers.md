### #Character 
## Overview
Agents of [[The Iron Reliquary]], they roam far corners of the land collecting rare, magical or valuable items, relics, or even rumors for trade/sale, or for [[The Collector]] themself. They operate under the guise of traders and/or historians.
All reclaimers have the **Detect Magic** and **Identify** traits
They also operate under a code:
* No misuse of the artifacts
* Never lie about provenance (origin/earliest known history)
* Collect items for their value alone
