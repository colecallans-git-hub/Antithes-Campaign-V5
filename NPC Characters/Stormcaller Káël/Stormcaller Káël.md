### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
A tiefling sorcerer living in isolation as a hermit on [[Bane's Rise]]. He is haunted by visions - either during great storms, or his visions may cause localized storms.
He is kind to wanderers, but distrustful/weary of authority.
## Hooks/Ties
Likes to meditate on Bane's Rise, he gladly offers safe haven to travelers. 
## Sheet
Neutral Good
Tiefling Sorcerer (storm sorcery)
**Stormstaff**: A +1 handmade staff that acts as a focus for storm spells
**Vial of Rainwater**: Healing potion equivalent
**Tattered robe**: A long cloak Káël is unwilling to part with - grants adv on saves against weather
**Storm Call**: Cast *Thunderwave* or *gust of wind* (DC14) without use of slots (3/day)
**Tempest Flight**: Fly 30ft during storms (concentration, 1 min.)
