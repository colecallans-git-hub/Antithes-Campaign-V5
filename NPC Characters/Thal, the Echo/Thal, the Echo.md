### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
A ghost of the [[Remains of Wray Keep]]. he was once a scholar who perished along with the keep, now trapped, melancholic yet still knowledgeable. 
## Hooks/Ties
He seemingly haunts PCs while in the keep, though if they approach him they find out he is not foe.
He may share fragments of his memories - bits and pieces that clue the PCs into how the keep was destroyed by the Veiled Assembly.
