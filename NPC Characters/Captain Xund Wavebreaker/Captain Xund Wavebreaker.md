### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
An older, experienced captain calloused by a lifetime at sea. He has sailed most of the waters between the continents and knows the [[Seas]] better than almost anyone. Outwardly jovial and fond of a good tale, he becomes serious and commanding when the situation demands it.

He grew up in [[Thurry Harbor]], becoming accustomed to their ways before eventually setting sail under his own captain before him.
He visited a few places around the world before he decided to start his own life and crew in [[Velport Haven]]. Here, he founded his reliable boat *Wavebreaker*.
## Hooks/Ties:
- Captain of reliable sailing vessels based in and around [[Seastone Hall]].
- Can ferry the party across seas and serves as their primary long-term sailing / fast-travel contact.
- Keeps a captain’s log of notable sights and a large hand-annotated map of the known world in his quarters.
- Has taken jobs transporting goods for pay, sometimes consisting of artifacts for [[The Iron Reliquary]].
- Takes Fin on as crew and becomes positively disposed toward the party after they help Fin.
- Owns a handful of ships, and may not always be the one to ferry the crew if they come across one of his ships.
## Sheet:
- Alignment: Neutral Good
- Race: Human
- Class/Archetype: Swashbuckler Rogue (or experienced sailor with rogue-like skills)
- Notable:
	- **Advantage** on *Vehicle (water)* checks; extensive knowledge of sea routes and weather
	- **Advantage** on *Survival* checks while sailing on water
