### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
A noble who once stood proud among the elite of the [[Palace of the Unworthy]]. Bitter and vengeful, he is now banished and left to scour the barren landscape.
Introduces himself as "Raël, the rightful king". He is well-known for being an ambidextrous fighter.
Most notably, he wields a greatsword that is blinding from the desert sun's reflection. It is enchanted with the exile's curse - dealing **1d6** psychic of extra damage to betrayers. His tattered indigo royal cloak is a symbol known to all who have heard his story. It grants him **adv** on intimidation while in [[Ūdeth]].

He also dons a large bracer that encases his entire left arm- from fingers to shoulder. Since his exile, he has lost weight and become a bit weaker, his bracer now swallowing his arm rather than fitting him.
## Hooks/Ties
Encounter PCs along Infinite Coastline or some place near the Badlands, offering intel on the Palace of the Unworthy in exchange for help regaining his power in some form or fashion.

Suspects his exile was orchestrated/manipulated by the Veiled Assembly. Upon discovery of the egg, he believes it to be a potential weapon against the Order, or the Assembly.
