### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
Raised by the Kokiri that live in the [[Kokiri Dreamglade]], he is a human that learned the ways and skills of the elves. Having lost his family at a young age to the [[Thornbrush Forest]], he now patrols the from the edges of the Thornbrush and [[Degano Lake]], and the [[Kokiri Shadowmere]]. He prioritizes survival, distrusting magic users and remaining vigilant and stealthy
## Hooks/Ties
Guides PCs through Wilds for a fee.
Spots Gloam Serpents near Kokiri Shadowmere
