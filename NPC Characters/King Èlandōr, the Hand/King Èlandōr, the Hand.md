### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
The king of the [[Lands of the Hand]], he rules inside of the [[North Hand]], residing in the [[Palace of the Hand]].
Ambitious and regal, with a calm demeanor that hides paranoia. He values unity through his vision of "loyalty". Actively pessimistic, he constantly searches for weaknesses and possible division. Sees the "new era" as a threat to his rule over the Hands unless controlled.
Though thought to be originally from the [[Land of Five]], specifically the North Hand, he is actually from [[Ūdeth]], hinted by the accents in his name. He has since removed the accents in his name to hide any suspicion about his heritage.
## Hooks/Ties
Messenger is sent to the party at some point, offering resources for safeguarding the group as well as the egg (though he demands tribute or fealty). He has an advisor inside of [[The Veiled Assembly]] that he is manipulated by without knowing, pushing [[Inward City]] aggression. He sees the egg as a potential issue that he wants to be able to control. If rumors of a second egg spread, he sees it as a tool for unification, but is weary of primordials threatening his coastal kingdom.
