## Features & Traits

> [!abstract]- Species Traits (Eladrin / Elf)
> **Ability Score Increase**  
> Dexterity +2, Intelligence +1
>
> **Darkvision**  
> 60 ft. (shades of gray)
>
> **Keen Senses**  
> Proficiency in Perception
>
> **Fey Ancestry**  
> Advantage on saves against being charmed; magic cannot put you to sleep
>
> **Trance**  
> Meditate 4 hours instead of sleeping 8 hours. Gain the same benefit as a long rest.
>
> **Elf Weapon Training**  
> Proficiency with longsword, shortsword, shortbow, and longbow
>
> **Fey Step**  
> Once per short rest, cast *Misty Step*

> [!abstract]- Class Features (Druid 1)
> **Druidic**  
> You know Druidic, the secret language of druids.
>
> **Spellcasting**  
> Wisdom is your spellcasting ability. You prepare spells from the Druid list. You use a Druidic Focus.

> [!abstract]- Background: Sage (Researcher)
> **Feature: Researcher**  
> When you attempt to learn or recall a piece of lore, if you do not know that information, you often know where and from whom you can obtain it. This information usually comes from a library, scriptorium, university, or a sage or other learned person or creature.
