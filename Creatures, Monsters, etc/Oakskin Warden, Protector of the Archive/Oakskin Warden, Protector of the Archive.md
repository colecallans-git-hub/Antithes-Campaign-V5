### #Character 
## Origin/Overview:
The guardian of the [[Verdant Archive]], it is a sentient [[treant]] given its life by the [[Sacred Tree Bodhisattva]]. So long as the Primordial lives, the Oakskin Warden is etched into history.
It is true neutral.
