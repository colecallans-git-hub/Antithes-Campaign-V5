#Primordial 
Thāl’veyra is a legendary sea serpent said to dwell only in the deepest parts of the world’s oceans.
It may later be revealed as (or connected to) the great water primordial.

Sailors claim it occasionally rises near the surface to feed, causing sudden and violent disturbances. It has been described as an extremely long snake, owning no appendages. However, it boasts a very long and large dorsal fin along the length of its back.
Those that have heard stories (as nobody has seen it in the flesh and lived) have relayed that its mouth is bigger than any ship in history, able to swallow crews whole. It's body is said to be what creates the world's currents as it moves beneath the crushing dark waters.
ON RARE OCCASION, it has been rumored that some have seen Thāl’veyra's body breaching onto land, looking so large that it seems to be part of the ground at first glance.
