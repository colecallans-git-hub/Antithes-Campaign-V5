The air primordial is directly representative of the winds of change and alteration.
This primordial is actually the smallest, yet arguably the most influential and commanding of the natural forces. It's almost as if the primordial does more than just dictate its realm surrounding its element—almost as if it sees and understands paths of predetermined choices: fate.

This primordial is also the Chronovane dragon, who we see perish at the beginning of the story to start things. The [[Bahamut's Egg]] is introduced, though the PCs do no know it is a primordial at all.
