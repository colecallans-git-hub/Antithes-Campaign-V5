 A group of upset consumers are heard talking in a group about about a poor vendor found on the docks of the waterfront that allegedly sells "counterfeit" or "junk" charms and wares. Several of his wares have no physical use outside of their appearance, and customers feel lead astray.
* He claims to sell 'wards' and 'Eastern Dragon-salt'. These few do have uses, though others do not
Investigate the vendor and his wares for more information.
* **(Optional):** Help protect the vendor from angry customers
* **(Optional):** Tell the angry customers he runs a scam
**Potential Rewards:** (not seen to the party)
* If assisted the angry customers:
	* The group gives you what they believe are useless knacks There are a few good items within the pile.
* If assisted the poor vendor:
	* He will give a **permanent discount** to the party (or those in support of his side), and find/hold on to better items to sell to the party on occasion.
	* **Free Dragon-salt** (allows the user to blow or throw the salt like dust/dirt from their hand, igniting it in the air. Can be useful for starting fires, close range combat, or very quick light emission/signals)
