### 🎭 BACKSTORY BASICS

2. Try to come up with what kind of personal connection(s) you would have to dragons. Dragons are present, so I would like to know your character's opinion on them.

3. Do you have an **opinion on battles** between warring states?

4. Would you have a **connection to any wars/battles if they were to occur (or occurred in the past)?** (soldier, freedom fighter, civilian caught in the middle, person in hiding?)

5. What's your *view/outlook* on it? (Do you trust those in charge? Hate wars? Want them to end or profit from it?)

6. Who are you *loyal* to?

7. Is your character *traveling* or *working away from their home? If so, *why?*

8. **Who** in the world might you *trust*? Or *look up to*? Or *fear*?

9. What’s something **your character is afraid of**, hates, or doesn’t understand?

10. (Optional) What's a **personal secret** or **burden** your character caries? 

11. (Optional) What’s one weird quirk, detail or habit about them that people might notice? Or might not notice?

### 🤝 GROUP-LEVEL CONNECTION
1. Do you know any other player characters already? If so, how?
   (Optional: write short backstory links together.)

3. In a more general sense, what would motivate you to travel with a group or associate with a group? (e.g. money, purpose, hiding, orders, curiosity) Essentially, what do you seek to gain when interacting with others?

4. What *main role* are you hoping your character will fulfill as part of a group?
   (e.g. Soldier-type, healer, scout, diplomat, tinkerer, etc.)

5. Why are you willing to work with this specific crew? *Are* you willing to? What might be keeping you around?

### 🧰 OTHER QUESTIONS/PERSONAL SECRETS

- Is there something about your past you're hiding?

- Do you have any secret goals or private missions? (Perhaps this and the previous question blend together?)

- What kind of story are you hoping/expecting? Are you wanting to move mountains, or just survive in this part of the world? Are you okay with serious moments, or do you prefer fun first?

- What kind of creatures, beasts, beings, and the like are you expecting/hoping to see in this world? This will give me a frame of reference for things to come.

- Part 2: How would you like to handle death, tone shifts, betrayal, and things of that nature? (e.g. take action outside the law, work with council, or perhaps stay to help a hurt civilization?) Or would you rather we avoid them more? 

- Part 3: And essentially, how "dark" would you like things to get?

- Will these character(s) meet a tragic fate? Or do they have plot armor? Or undecided? (just want to know your preference)
