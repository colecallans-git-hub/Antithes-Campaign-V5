**Arc**: 1 – The Fallen Herald  
**Level**: 1  
**Location**: Sea route from [[Seastone Hall]] → Stormwreck Isle  
**Estimated Play Time**: 3–4 hours  
**Travel Time**: 3 days by sea (~180–220 miles)

## Session Goal
The party travels from [[Seastone Hall]] to Stormwreck Isle (Potential stop at Port Esely), experiences the journey, and arrives on the island ready to begin searching for the fallen dragon.

## Starting Situation
- The party has secured passage (via Mirene Thalor’s contact, Finn, or another arrangement from Session 1).
- They board the vessel in [[Seastone Hall]] harbor and depart.

## Key Beats

### Day 1 – Departure & Open Water
- Leaving [[Seastone Hall]] harbor. Describe the town shrinking behind them and the shift to open sea.
- Roleplay opportunity with the crew or among the party (first real chance for the group to talk if they started separate).
- Optional light skill use: helping with sails, navigation, or simply observing the waters.
- End of day: calm seas, first night aboard.

### Day 2 – The Middle Crossing
- The voyage continues. Weather can stay fair or turn slightly rough (your choice based on pacing).
- **Possible minor event** (choose one or none):
  - Distant dragon sighting high in the clouds (too far to engage, but raises tension).
  - Small group of sea birds acting strangely (foreshadowing the egg’s pull).
  - Brief encounter with a curious sea creature or floating debris from an older wreck.
- Night watch roleplay or quiet conversation.
- Binary / soft decision point (optional):  
  The party can choose to push harder through the night (arrive slightly earlier, risk exhaustion) or keep a normal pace.

### Day 3 – Approach & Arrival
- Stormwreck Isle comes into view: rocky, windswept, with visible signs of recent damage along the southwestern shore (broken trees, scorched earth, unusual ash drifts).
- Landing options:
  - Main natural harbor / beach (safer, more obvious).
  - Rougher secondary cove closer to the crash signs (riskier landing, shorter walk later).
- **Binary Decision Point**:  
  - Land at the safer beach → longer walk to the crash site, lower immediate risk.  
  - Attempt the closer, rougher cove → shorter path inland, higher chance of a difficult landing or immediate encounter.
- Party steps onto Stormwreck Isle. Session ends as they get their bearings and begin moving inland toward the signs of the fallen dragon.

## Possible Encounters / Events
- Light weather challenge (Athletics or Survival checks to stay secure on deck).
- Distant dragon silhouette (pure atmosphere).
- Minor environmental hazard on landing if they choose the rougher cove.

## DM Notes
- Use the three days to let the party bond and to slowly build a sense that something unusual is happening in the region.
- Keep combat light or absent unless the players seek it — this session is mostly travel, roleplay, and rising tension.
- Have a simple map of the southwestern coast of Stormwreck Isle ready (beach, rocky cove, tree line, distant smoke or ash plume).

## Rewards / Progress
- Safe arrival on Stormwreck Isle
- First clear visual evidence of the dragon crash
- Level up from 1 to 3 to take place after finding dragon.
