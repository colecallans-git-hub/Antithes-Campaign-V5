**Arc**: 1 – The Fallen Herald  
**Level**: 1  
**Location**: [[Seastone Hall]] (Northwest coast of Varaxes)  
**Estimated Play Time**: 3-4 hours

## Session Goal
The party hears the rumor of the fallen dragon, accepts (or is pulled into) the task of investigating Stormwreck Isle, and arranges passage there.

## Starting Situation
- Player characters begin **separate or in pairs**, all present in [[Seastone Hall]] at the same time.
- Suggested starting locations within the town: the docks, the main tavern (The Salted Scale), the market square, or near the notice board.

## Key Beats
1. **Introduction & Atmosphere**  
   Describe [[Seastone Hall]]: weathered coastal town, salt air, mix of sailors, traders, and locals who keep a wary eye on the sea and sky. Dragons are a real concern here.

2. **The Rumor Spreads**  
   The party overhears or is told about a large dragon seen falling from the sky onto Stormwreck Isle two nights ago. Details are incomplete and already being exaggerated.

3. **The Offer**  
   Mirene Thalor (local scholar / minor official / merchant contact) approaches one or more of the characters. She wants confirmation of the crash and any recoverable materials or information. She offers modest payment + potential future work.

4. **Binary Decision Point**  
   - **Accept the job** → Mirene helps arrange a boat and gives a small advance.  
   - **Refuse or ignore** → A secondary hook triggers (a desperate sailor who saw the fall, a competing offer from a shady merchant, or a public notice that other groups are already preparing to go).  
   This keeps the story moving even if the party is cautious.

5. **Gathering Information & Preparing**  
   - Optional skill checks (Investigation, History, Arcana, Persuasion) to learn more about Stormwreck Isle and recent dragon activity.  
   - Arrange passage (hire a boat, work for passage, or use Mirene’s contact).

6. **Session End**  
   The party is either on the boat and leaving the harbor, or has finalized arrangements and will depart at first light.

## Important NPCs
- **[[Mirene Thalor]]** – Practical, slightly anxious, knowledgeable about local waters and old stories. Wants answers more than treasure.
- **[[Fin the Dockhand]]** (optional early side character) – Dockhand who can offer a better boat option in exchange for a favor.

## DM Notes
- Keep the tone grounded and slightly tense. This is the calm before the party realizes what they’ve stepped into.
- Let the players roleplay introductions naturally while the rumor and offer pull them together.
- Have one backup boat option ready in case they reject Mirene’s help.

## Rewards / Progress
- Quest accepted (or alternative hook triggered)
- Passage to Stormwreck Isle secured
- Possible small advance (10–15 gp total) or minor favors
