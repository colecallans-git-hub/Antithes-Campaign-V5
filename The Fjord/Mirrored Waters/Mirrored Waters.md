#Bodyofwater 
A mix between the [[Tundra's Abyss]], [[The Touched Bay]] and the [[Antithes Sea]], these are the calmer waters that surround the western portion of the land.
Air is pushed Northeast from the Touched Sea up into the Sea of the Fjord, and over towards [[The Fjord]], creating its notable frigid and glassy looking waters, paired with calm lapping waves.
