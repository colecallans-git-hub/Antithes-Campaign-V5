## Region
[[Seastone Hall]] (northern Swordcoast Islands, Varaxes)

## Description
The local counting house and office of **The Red Seal** in Seastone Hall. Here merchants, captains, and travelers can exchange currency, arrange larger transactions, and deal with promissory notes backed by the House of the Golden Quill.

The building is more formal and orderly than most of the surrounding dockside structures. Inside are ledgers, scales, sealed strongboxes, and clerks who measure both metal and reputation with equal care. A red wax seal is prominently displayed.

## Key NPCs
- Clerks and agents of [[The Red Seal]]
- Occasional presence of [[The Black Seal]] when overdue notes need attention

## Secrets
- This office is one of the cleaner sources of information about who currently holds large debts or valuable notes in the city.
- Promissory notes issued or honored here carry the full weight of The Red Seal’s authority.
