## Region
[[Seastone Hall]] (northern Swordcoast Islands, Varaxes)

## Description
A well-known, working-class tavern and eatery near the docks of [[Seastone Hall]]. The Dirty Gull serves as a reliable gathering place for sailors, dockworkers, merchants, and travelers. It is not fancy, but it is clean enough, well-run, and consistently busy.

The tavern primarily serves drinks — ale, beer, bottles of wine, and rum — along with simple meals made from a few reliable ingredients. Seafood (including large crab when available) appears regularly. The kitchen also sells whole ingredients such as cheese, bread, and seasonal fruits and vegetables when supply is high.

The atmosphere is lively but controlled. Rook keeps a firm hand on the place, and serious trouble is usually shut down quickly.

## Key NPCs
- [[Maris “Rook” Calder]] — Owner and primary barkeep (Sea Elf)
- [[Tam “Twig” Vellin]] — Secondary barkeep and kitchen help (Halfling)
- [[Mirene Thalor]] —Can be found here on occasion, looking for work/jobs

## Secrets
- Rook hears a great deal of dockside rumor and quietly filters what she shares.
- The tavern is considered relatively neutral ground by most regulars; starting major violence here is widely frowned upon.
- Because of its location and steady traffic, the Dirty Gull is a natural place for the party to pick up information, meet contacts, or simply rest between voyages.
