#SeastoneHall #Landmark #city 
## Region
Northernmost island of the [[Swordcoast Islands]], directly west of the tip of [[Cape Hartbay]] (Varaxes)

## Description
One of the largest and busiest port cities in Antithes, Seastone Hall is a major hub of trade, information, and daily life. Though often called a fishing city, the majority of its goods come through import and export, bringing food and materials from across the known world.

The city is built primarily of laid stone and stone brick, with occasional stone used in the street paving. Many of the permanent buildings use dark blues and dark greens in their construction. Closer to the docks, moss and algae creep along the ground, retaining walls, and lower building walls, though the streets themselves are kept relatively clean. A constant sea breeze moves through the city.

The harbor contains docks capable of handling ships of all sizes. The atmosphere is always busy and in motion. Weather is usually fair — ranging from sunny to overcast — with frequent humidity and the occasional storm.

While the city has some defenses, large-scale violence is uncommon. It is generally understood that Seastone Hall is not a place to bring serious trouble; those who do quickly find themselves opposed by many capable people. Smaller scraps still occur.

Key locations within the city include the main docks, the hall of **[[The Aēlthôra]]**, various vendor stalls and warehouses, and the party’s regular tavern, *The Dirty Gull*.

## Secrets
- As a major information hub, Seastone Hall quietly collects rumors from across the continents. Certain people (including Mirene and Rook) hear more than they usually share.
- The constant flow of ships makes it relatively easy for individuals or small groups to arrive or leave without drawing heavy attention — for better or worse.
