## Region
[[Seastone Hall]] (northern Swordcoast Islands, Varaxes)

## Description
The headquarters of **[[The Aēlthôra]]**, the dock and harbor guild of Seastone Hall. From this hall the guild manages docking rights, harbor fees, loading permissions, and related debts.

The building is practical and solidly built, reflecting the working nature of the organization. Inside are offices, record rooms, and spaces where captains and merchants come to settle business. [[Mirene Thalor]] is frequently found here or nearby.

## Key NPCs
- [[Mirene Thalor]] — Senior figure at the Seastone location within The Aēlthôra
- Various guild clerks, officers, and dock representatives

## Secrets
- The hall contains detailed records of ships, docking accounts, and outstanding harbor debts.
- Because so much of the port’s daily business flows through it, Aēlthôra Hall is one of the better places in the city to learn who has recently arrived or departed by sea.
