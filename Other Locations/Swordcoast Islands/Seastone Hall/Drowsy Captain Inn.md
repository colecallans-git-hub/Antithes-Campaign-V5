## Region
[[Seastone Hall]] (northern Swordcoast Islands, Varaxes)

## Description
A solid, mid-range inn closer to the center of Seastone Hall. The Drowsy Captain offers clean rooms, decent beds, and a large yet quiet enough common area for travelers who need rest between voyages. It is popular with sailors, merchants, and adventurers who want something more reliable than a shared floor in a tavern.

Looking closer to a small hotel, the inn is practical rather than luxurious. Rooms are simple but well-kept, and the staff are used to dealing with people who arrive at odd hours and leave just as quickly.
