## Region
[[Seastone Hall]] (northern Swordcoast Islands, Varaxes)

## Description
A well-stocked general goods store that sits a step above the open market stalls of Seastone Hall. True to its name, Home to Harbor sells a wide range of practical wares — everything a person might need from daily life ashore, to the requirements of life on the docks and at sea.

Shelves and bins hold tools, travel supplies, basic gear, cooking goods, rope, lanterns, and other reliable merchandise. The shop is somewhat disorganized but reasonably priced, and frequented by both locals and travelers preparing for a voyage.
