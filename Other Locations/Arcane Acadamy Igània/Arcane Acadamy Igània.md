#WonderOfTheWorld #Landmark 
This island lies to the East of [[Varaxes]], hidden in a natural sense. It is difficult to reach this place due to its location between the two continents of [[Ūdeth]] and Varaxes, near the southern end of the [[Merchant's Pass]].

This is a place for scholars, who in past history have placed enchantments to better protect and hide the place from outsiders. Being so secretive, and with how grandiose it is, this makes it one of the [[Ten Wonders of the World]].
