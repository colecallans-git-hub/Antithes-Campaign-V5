#item 
The egg of Bahamut is a very rare platinum dragon, a symbol of [[The Old World]] and a herald of change. The egg, about twice the size of a football and heavy, is coated in scales like a dragon. It has a pearlescent coloring, a gray steel color with tinges of red around the edges of its scales. But in more direct sunlight, it glows a whiter color, and the edges of its scales turn green.

---

_Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement)_

This large, faintly luminous egg is warm to the touch and seems to pulse with a slow, living rhythm. It is a vessel for the reincarnating essence of a Chronovane dragon.

**Attunement.** Attuning to the Egg requires 2 consecutive days of focused contact. While attuning, if you take 3 or more damage from a single source, you must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 12). On a failure, you drop the egg and the attunement attempt ends; you must begin the process again.

**Properties while attuned.**

- The egg feels significantly lighter to you (treat its weight as half).
- You gain resistance to extreme environmental heat and cold.

**Handling by non-attuned creatures.**  
Any creature that is not attuned to the egg must succeed on a Strength check (DC 15) to pick it up or lift it. On a failure, the creature cannot lift the egg and gains 1 level of exhaustion. The creature may attempt the check again, but each failure applies another level of exhaustion.

Only one creature can be attuned to the egg at a time. Another creature may begin the attunement process (willingly or otherwise), but the one currently attuned can feel when someone else tries. Once someone else completes the attunement process, the original person will lose attunement, and will no longer feel the egg's presence.
