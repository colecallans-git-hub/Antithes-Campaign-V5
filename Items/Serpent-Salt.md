*Wondrous item, common*

This coarse, reddish-gray salt crystals faintly sparkle and carry a sharp, metallic scent. A small pouch typically contains enough for 5 uses.

As an action, you can throw or blow a pinch of Serpent-Salt into the air within 5 feet of yourself. The salt ignites in a brief, bright flash. Choose one of the following effects:

- **Ignite.** You can light a flammable object (torch, campfire, oil, etc.) within 5 feet.  
- **Signal.** The flash emits bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet until the start of your next turn.  
- **Burn.** One creature within 5 feet must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw (DC 12) or take 1d4 fire damage.
	- This can be thrown into the face of a creature if specified. If successful, it receives the **blinded** status effect until the end of its next turn.

Once the salt is used, that pinch is consumed.
