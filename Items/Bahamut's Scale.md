_Wondrous item, uncommon

This single iridescent scale is cool and smooth, yet occasionally flickers with faint internal light.
This item does not require attunement.
- You can use it as a spellcasting focus.
- **Absorbing Ward.** Once per long rest, you can cast _Absorb Elements_ without expending a spell slot.
- **Presence of the Scale.** While you are holding or wearing the scale, any creature of size Small or smaller that can see the scale must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 13) or become frightened of you until the start of its next turn. A creature that succeeds on this saving throw is immune to this effect for 24 hours.
