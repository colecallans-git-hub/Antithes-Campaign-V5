Magic in Antithes is drawn from the lingering echoes of the Old World cataclysm and the stirring Primordials. It is **visible**, **socially stigmatized**, and requires deliberate channeling.

## Core Principles
- All magic produces a noticeable visual display (glowing runes, elemental particles, swirling energy, etc.).
- Public casting draws attention, suspicion, or hostility in most civilized areas.
- Magic is not subtle. Society has been conditioned to view overt magic with wariness.

---

## Casting Methods

### 1. Focus Casting (Primary Method)
- **Required** for all spells of **3rd level and higher**.
- The caster must hold a physical spellcasting focus (wand, staff, orb, crystal, amulet, etc.).
- The focus replaces:
  - The **somatic** component
  - Any material component that has **no gold cost** and is **not consumed**
- Expensive or consumed materials can be attached to (or kept in close proximity to) the focus in any fashion the caster prefers — mounted, hung from a keychain-style attachment, worn as nearby jewelry, placed in a socket, etc. Once properly associated with the focus, they count as available for the relevant spells.
- Foci are highly personal. Casters often customize them in whatever way best suits their style and the components they need.

### 2. Somatic Casting (Hand Signs)
- Allowed only for **cantrips through 2nd-level spells**.
- No focus is required.
- The caster performs precise, visible hand signs.
- The caster must still provide the **specific material component(s)** listed in the spell description (if any).
- Hand signs are very noticeable and still draw social attention.

### Verbal Component
- Required for **both** methods.
- The caster must be able to speak clearly.

---

## Material Components Summary

| Component Type              | Focus Casting                                      | Somatic Casting (≤ 2nd level)                  |
|----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| No cost, not consumed      | Replaced by focus                                  | Must provide the component listed in the spell |
| Has gold cost or consumed  | Must be attached/associated with focus or provided | Must provide the component listed in the spell |

---

## Practical Notes for Play
- Losing or breaking a focus is a serious setback for any caster of 3rd level or higher.
- Story items (e.g. Chronovane Scale, Old World relics, primordial shards) often function as exceptional foci and may have extra capacity or unique properties for associating components.
- Components associated with a focus remain visible and can be targeted, stolen, or destroyed.
- The visual display and social stigma apply regardless of which method is used.
- Only the material component(s) actually listed in a given spell’s description are ever required.
