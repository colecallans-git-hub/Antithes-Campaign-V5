#Faction/Cult/Guild/Clan 
A cult-like group that believes death is merely the beginning of one's true form. They worship the primordial entity Essuul, the Wyrm Beneath.
They have been seen to operate in the [[Bowels of the Blight]] and similar places. 
They have inklings of a dragon egg that they plan to try to use to summon their deity.
Their magic is slippery - based on whispers, shadows, and unseen influence. Members can appear as helpful strangers or sages/hermits, until they vanish.
