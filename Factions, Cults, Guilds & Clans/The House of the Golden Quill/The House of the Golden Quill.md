#Faction/Cult/Guild/Clan 
## Type

Wealthy Clan / Financial Backing House

## Overview

An old and highly influential clan of significant wealth. The House of the Golden Quill provides the ultimate financial backing and legitimacy for the banking system of Antithes. Their reserves, held largely in Spire-Silver (Dragon-Silver), underwrite the promissory notes issued by *The Red Seal*.

## Role in the World

- Ultimate guarantors of major financial instruments
- Maintain large private reserves of Spire-Silver
- Rarely involve themselves in day-to-day collections or small debts
- Represent old money, stability, and long-term financial power

## Hooks / Ties

- Back [[The Red Seal]] and, by extension, [[The Black Seal]]
- Their name and reputation give weight to high-value promissory notes
- May occasionally take interest in extremely large or politically sensitive debts
- Connected to the highest levels of commerce and certain noble houses

## Symbol

A quill rendered in gold, often set within a circular border. Sometimes it can be seen as just a quill.

## Attitude Toward Adventurers

Distant and selective. They rarely deal with individuals directly unless the sums or political implications are substantial.
