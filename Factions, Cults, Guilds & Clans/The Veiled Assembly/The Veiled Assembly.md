#Faction/Cult/Guild/Clan 
Disguised as political advisors, scribes and sages, they move to manipulate events across the world behind closed doors. Their members are of the Githzerai species.
Members of its group are highly trained, though not in direct combat. They excel in manipulation, historic lore and tactile thinking. Many rulers see them as extremely wise and use them as counselors, unknowingly (or possibly even willingly) taking their advice.

They have ties to [[The Old World]], now lost, as well as the downfall of many other groups that have since lived. They are one of the only living things that perseveres to this day.
