#Faction/Cult/Guild/Clan 
## Type
Banking Guild

## Overview
The primary banking guild of Antithes. *The Red Seal* issues promissory notes, manages accounts, and handles legitimate financial operations in major ports and cities. Their notes are backed by [[The House of the Golden Quill]].

## Role in the World
- Issue and honor promissory notes
- Record debts, loans, and contracts
- Provide a trusted medium of exchange for larger transactions so that heavy metal does not need to be carried
- Maintain offices or agents in significant coastal towns and trade centers

## The Seal Magic
When a promissory note is first stamped, the wax seal appears **red**, indicating the note is valid. Over time, or when a listed due date passes, the seal darkens from the center outward until it becomes fully **black**. A black seal marks the note as overdue.

## Symbol
A crystalline cluster rendered in red within a circular wax-seal border. The same mark is used by The Black Seal in black.

## Hooks / Ties
- Directly supported by *The House of the Golden Quill*
- Employs [[The Black Seal]] for collection and enforcement
- Minimum metal normally accepted on their notes is Copper/Bronze
- Present in [[Seastone Hall]] and most major ports

## Attitude Toward Adventurers
Professional and transactional. They care that ledgers balance and that notes are honored.
