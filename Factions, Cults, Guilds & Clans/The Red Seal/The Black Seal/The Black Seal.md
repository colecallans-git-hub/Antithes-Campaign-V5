#Faction/Cult/Guild/Clan 
## Type
Collection & Enforcement Arm

## Overview
The enforcement and collection branch of [[The Red Seal]]. *The Black Seal* handles overdue promissory notes and the sharper side of debt recovery. When a seal on a note turns fully black, it effectively becomes a warrant that *The Black Seal* may act upon.

## Role in the World
- Pursue overdue debts and black-sealed notes
- Act as the visible, practical arm of the banking system in ports and trade towns
- Employ a mix of clerks, tracers, and more direct enforcers
- Prefer clean resolution but are prepared to apply pressure when necessary

## Symbol
The same crystalline cluster used by *The Red Seal*, rendered in black within a circular border.

## Visual Identifiers
- Practical dark clothing
- Black seal pendant, stamped leather bracer, or dark metal pin bearing the crystalline mark
- Often carry a small locked ledger or a set of scales

## Hooks / Ties
- Operates under the authority of *The Red Seal*
- Ultimately backed by the reputation of [[The House of the Golden Quill]]
- May come into contact with adventurers who hold (or have failed to honor) promissory notes
- Present in [[Seastone Hall]] and other active ports

## Attitude Toward Adventurers
Neutral to stern. They are concerned with the status of notes and debts rather than personal grudges. A party that resolves matters cleanly is left alone; a party that ignores black-sealed obligations will eventually draw attention.
