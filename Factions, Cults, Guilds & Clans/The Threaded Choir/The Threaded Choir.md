#Faction/Cult/Guild/Clan 
A following based on beliefs around fatalism and destiny.
The universe is bound by threads of fate, and the Choir seeks to pull those threads to influence destiny in their favor. They see themselves as instruments of inevitability.

Though only its most elite belong to the actionable Choir, it is still more of a widespread/known idea, and few choose to follow it through a devout path. Many of those that may be found belonging to the Choir are those that live squalid lives, hoping to find a more positive outlook on life, or credit their outcome to destiny. Few people in positions of high power belong to the Choir and credit their success to taking action over their fate.
Those in positions of power may operate in the open, subtly manipulating politics ands war in favor of the rest of The Threaded Choir.
