#Faction/Cult/Guild/Clan 
## Type
Dock & Harbor Guild

## Overview
The primary dock and harbor guild of [[Seastone Hall]]. The Aēlthôra oversees docking rights, harbor fees, loading permissions, and related debts. The name carries the sense of a sea breeze in the Ūdeth tongue. Practical and influential within the port, the guild keeps the daily business of the docks running.

## Role in the World
- Manage docking rights and harbor fees in [[Seastone Hall]]
- Oversee loading and unloading permissions
- Track and collect dock-related debts
- Serve as the main authority for captains, merchants, and dockworkers operating in the port

## Hooks / Ties
- [[Mirene Thalor]] holds a senior position within the guild
- Regularly interacts with independent captains (including [[Captain Xund Wavebreaker]]) and larger trading groups such as [[The Blue Lantern Company]]
- Maintains working relationships with financial interests in the port, including contacts connected to [[The Red Seal]]
- Can become a recurring source of local work and information whenever the party is in [[Seastone Hall]]

## Attitude Toward Adventurers
Professional and pragmatic. They value those who keep agreements and cause minimal disruption on the docks. Reliability earns favor; chaos or unpaid obligations earn coldness.
