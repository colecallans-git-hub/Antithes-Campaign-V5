#IdeaInProgress #Faction/Cult/Guild/Clan 
This group believes themselves wardens of a new order, hoping to see the other side of a world soon to be covered in fire and ash, born anew.
They reside in the [[Palace of the Unworthy]], one of the largest organized followings among other factions.
They rank amongst themselves in a hierarchy:
***Cinder Acolytes*** are among the lowest
***Flame Wardens*** don't necessarily rank higher (though they can), but they act as enforcers
***Ash Prophets*** rank among the higher end of the hierarchy, typically remaining in the Palace of the Unworthy.
