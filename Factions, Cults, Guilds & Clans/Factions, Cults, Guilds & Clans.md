#Index 
**Faction:** Shared ideals, politics, or beliefs—Broader social, ideological, or governmental goals—Political parties, rebel alliances, religious orders
**Guild:** Shared profession, trade, or skill—Job support, trading standards, or specific activities—Blacksmiths' guild, Mages' guild, gaming PvE group
**Clan:** Kinship, family ties, or close bonds—Mutual protection, tight-knit identity, and competitive play—Scottish clans, gaming esports rosters
