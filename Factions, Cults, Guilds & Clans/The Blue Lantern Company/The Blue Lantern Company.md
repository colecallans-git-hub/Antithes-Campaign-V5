#Faction/Cult/Guild/Clan 
## Type: Trading & Shipping Guild
## Overview:
A well-known and relatively respected merchant guild that coordinates trade voyages, cargo contracts, and shipping insurance across the major ports of Antithes. Originating out of the Land of Five, their symbol is a stylized blue lantern, said to represent safe passage through dark waters. They maintain offices or agents in most significant coastal towns, including [[Seastone Hall]].
## Role in the World:
- Arrange and insure merchant voyages
- Hire reliable captains and crews
- Act as middlemen between producers, merchants, and ship owners
- Generally prefer stability and predictable profits over risk
## Hooks/Ties:
- May post legitimate transport or escort jobs
- Can serve as a more “official” alternative to independent captains
- Have occasional tension with smaller independent operators and with groups that deal in less reputable cargo
## Attitude Toward Adventurers:
Professional and transactional. They will hire competent groups when it serves their interests, but they are cautious about anyone who brings too much trouble to their docks or ships.
