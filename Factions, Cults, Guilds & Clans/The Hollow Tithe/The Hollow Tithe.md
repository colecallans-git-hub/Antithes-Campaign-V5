#Faction/Cult/Guild/Clan 
This group doesn't exactly congregate, but individuals practice. They worship something that takes but never gives, partaking in things such as starving rituals, vows of silence or sacrifices of some sort. 
This group believes the world must be emptied to be made whole. Members slowly give up parts of themselves- possessions, memories, identity. The practices leave members hollow-eyed, speaking in riddles/incoherently, or even voiceless.
They often operate shrines disguised as hospices or places of sanctuary.
