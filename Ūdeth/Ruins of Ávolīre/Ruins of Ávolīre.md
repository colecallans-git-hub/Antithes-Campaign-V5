#Landmark 
What is left of the great kingdom of Ávolīre is the greatest evidence of the era of [[The Old World]], as well as a great mystery to its downfall.

Located East of [[The Chasm]] on [[Ūdeth]], The ruins can be seen easily as they stretch on for miles, scorched and leveled by what looks like dragon-fire.
The city looks to have pre-dated Ūdeth's regression to the deserted [[Badlands]], suggesting that the deserts now surrounding the ruins only started after the fall of Ávolīre- or as a result of it.

The Kingdom of Ávolīre built the [[Great Library of the Old World]] to store its knowledge, eventually becoming what was known as the largest library in [[Antithes]]. Along with the Great Library, Ávolīre built the [[Royal Keep of the Old World]] (though then it was only the royal keep). This alone is said to be the size of an entire city, connected to the northern-most section of the entire massive kingdom of Ávolīre. The rest of Ávolīre swallows other known cities and kingdoms due to its size.
