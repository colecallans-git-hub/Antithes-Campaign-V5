#Landmark #stronghold 
This stronghold of foul creatures is located just above [[Maker’s Peak]] in the [[Badlands]].

Many creatures from here come looking for refuge from either [[The Chasm]] or the [[Bowels of the Blight]]. This blend of creatures mixed with the environment they grow up in gives rise to unique monsters.

These monsters are usually in contest with those in the [[Palace of the Unworthy]], located on the other side of Maker's Peak.
