#Landmark 
Centuries ago, the dwarven ancestors that founded the [[Shatterstone Citadel]] had to fight in order to claim what is now their home. The vale in the [[Adamantine Highlands]] is named after the commander of the dwarven army, who then became the first king of the citadel.

The story of the fight at the vale begins when the dwarves find the mountain range, finding it fit for civilization. [[The Chasm]], South of the highlands, was wrought with foul beast that had chased them through the northern Badlands up into the vale. Finding positional advantage, they rallied against their pursuers and defended against their seemingly endless advance.

Today, this vale sits just in between (filler mountain peak) and [[Commander's Peak]], and grows loose grass for the wildlife, and blooms beautiful flowers found nowhere else that need the areas wet climate. It is said the bodies fallen in the battle cause the flowers to bloom, fertilizing the land for thousands of years.
