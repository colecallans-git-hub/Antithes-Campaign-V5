#Mountain 
Commander's Peak is the southern most mountain peak in the [[Adamantine Highlands]]. It is the 4th tallest in the highlands. 

The battle at [[Commander's Vale]] eventually ended when both sides looked upon the peak when both stars shone brightly on its apex as they began to meet the horizon. There they saw the leaders of both armies, engaged in combat. Eventually, the dwarven commander bested his fiendish foe, and legend tells of a stone greatsword- said to be the sword of the commander- chiseled out of the stone resting at the platform on the peak at which they fought.
