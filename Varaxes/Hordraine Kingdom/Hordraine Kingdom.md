#kingdom 
The only surviving civilization of [[The Old World]], this Elven kingdom feared the dangers of what seemed to have taken place among the destruction of [[The Old World]], and alienated themselves from the rest of the world. Through these efforts, they survived- though nobody except for them seems to know the truth.

A large kingdom located on [[Varaxes]], they are slightly more customary than other areas of the world, seemingly due to their seclusion.
