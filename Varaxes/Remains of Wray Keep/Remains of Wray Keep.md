#Landmark 
What remains here is little but rubble now, but it is one of the few pieces of proof of [[The Old World]].

Wray Keep used to bee a hub for wizards, mages, and scholars alike would commune to gather/exchange information. In time, this practice made it one of the largest collections of knowledge in the world.

Located on [[Varaxes]], it is believed that the wizards seen to have been spotted in the Elderwood were the wizards that managed to find refuge there after the keep's destruction.
