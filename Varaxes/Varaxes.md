#
One of the [[Continents]] of [[Antithes]]. 
A triangular shaped continent in the latitudinal center of the world ([[Antithes]]), with temperate climate full of many different species of trees. It is mainly covered in thick forest, home to wild flora and fauna. These wooded areas that span the continent almost in its entirety are called the [[Faron Woodlands]].

The entire winding west coast contains sandy white beaches, blending into the gray sand of the Hartbay Coastline. It has many coastal cities along with islands around the bay.
