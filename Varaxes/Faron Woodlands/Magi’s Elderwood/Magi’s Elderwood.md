#Forest 
One of the many forests of the [[Faron Woodlands]]. The Elderwood extends East of the [[Faron Wilds]], said to be home to many mages and practitioners of the arts of magic.

Though these woods seem relatively normal, some have rumored to have seen solitary figures that seem like wizards who may protect the flora and fauna of the lands. Others believe they have seen them polymorphed as animals (different, or maybe an iteration of the druids of the Faron Wilds).
