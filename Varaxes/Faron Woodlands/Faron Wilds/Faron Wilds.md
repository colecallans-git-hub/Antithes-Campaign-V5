#Forest 
One of the southern forests in the [[Faron Woodlands]] of [[Varaxes]]. Southeast of the [[Kokiri Dreamglade]], these are connected through the [[Sacred Tree Bodhisattva]]. The sacred tree charges this forest with wild energy, giving rise powerful druids. Also bleeding into the Southern Dragonlands, the wilds are host to all manner of wild creatures and even dragon.

In its southern area, reaching into the [[Southern Dragonlands]] is the [[Half Moon Reservoir]].

It is also home to the [[Verdant Archive]], protected by druids and holding many secrets.
