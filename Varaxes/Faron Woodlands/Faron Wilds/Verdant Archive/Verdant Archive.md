#
An ancient grove located in the [[Faron Wilds]] where history is etched into the bark and growth rings of massive, sapient trees. This place, along with the rest of the wilds, is protected by druids. Those who protect the living library share its knowledge only with those who prove their harmony with nature- honoring it or presenting proof of noble intent. 

This place is older than any one person knows, its knowledge stored in its growing age. It is watched and protected by an ancient, moss-covered stone guardian.
