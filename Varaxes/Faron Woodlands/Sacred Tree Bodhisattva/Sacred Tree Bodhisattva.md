#WonderOfTheWorld #Primordial
Deep in the heart of the forest, somewhere between the [[Kokiri Dreamglade]] and the [[Faron Wilds]] lies one of the [[Ten Wonders of the World]] the [[Sacred Tree Bodhisattva|Sacred Tree Bhisattva]].

This is an [[Elder Tree]], easily the biggest tree on [[Varaxes]]. It is a mystical place difficult to reach for those not native to the lands. In a way, it is connected to the rest of the [[Faron Woodlands]] through powerful magic and is a great spiritual hotspot. The woods seem almost alive with the spirit of the forest because [[Sacred Tree Bodhisattva]] is a [[Primordials]] of the woodlands and nature.
