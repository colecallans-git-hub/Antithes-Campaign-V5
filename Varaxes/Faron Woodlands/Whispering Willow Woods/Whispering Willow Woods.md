#Forest 
The northern-most forest of the [[Faron Woodlands]] in [[Varaxes]], it consists mainly of willow tree, invoking a feeling of sorrow. Inhabitants and wanderers in the foods say the trees whisper on the wind as a way to communicate with one another, and ward beings away with their eerie murmurs.

Notable characteristics:
This forest marks the end of the [[Cape Hartbay]] area.
The deepest lake in Varaxes, [[Deep Springs Lake]] resides here.
A large clearing in the forest earned the name [[Hero's Clearing]] during the era of The Old World.
